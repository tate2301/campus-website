Data ownership

Your school's data stays yours.

Last updated 1 October 2026

The data belongs to the school

Everything the school puts into Campus belongs to the school. We hold it only to run the service.

Export everything, at any time

The Head or the bursar can export the whole record: pupils, guardians, staff, marks, fees and payroll, in files Excel and other systems open.

If you leave

Your export stays available until you confirm you have it. We then delete the school's data from Campus.

Who sees what

Each person sees what their role needs, and every change records who made it and when.

Backups

Campus is backed up every day, so a mistake or a lost device does not lose the school's records.

Offline copies

A device keeps what its user needs to work without internet. It syncs when the connection returns.

AI

AI features read only the school's own material. Each use is logged, and the school can switch AI off.
