## The line
**A school management platform that puts people first.**
Software for every department in your school, with one record at the centre.

## What Campus is
- Cloud-based school management software for K-12 schools, ECD to Upper Sixth.
- One record per person: each pupil, guardian and member of staff has one record that every department reads and adds to. A single source of truth for the school.
- It works without internet; changes sync when the connection returns.
- Departments (the Veracross split, our words): Academics and student life · Admissions and enrolment · Fees and billing · Accounting · HR and payroll · Boarding and welfare · Stock and facilities · Communication · Insights.
- Integrations: Sage Pastel, QuickBooks, ZIMRA fiscalisation (FDMS).
- Portals: Administration, Teacher, Parent, Student. Portals answer "who uses it"; departments answer "what it runs".

## Who we serve
- By school type (K-12): day, boarding, private, government, mission. Primary and secondary.
- By role: school leadership (Head, deputies, SDC or board), the bursary, teachers, parents and guardians, pupils.

## Moving from another system
- We move a school off its current SIS, Excel sheets or paper registers inside the four-week setup: two weeks to collect, map and import, two weeks of training at the school, then support through the term.
- What moves: pupils and guardians, staff and payroll details, fee structures and opening balances, marks and past reports, stock lists.
- The bursar signs off opening balances before go-live. The old system stays readable until the school switches it off. Data capture is included.
