The service

Corelith Labs provides Corelith Campus, cloud-based school management software, to the school named in its order.

Price

Campus is US$1 per active pupil per month. Campus with LMS and e-library is US$8.99 per active pupil per month and replaces the US$1 rate.

Billing

An active pupil is a learner on the roll in the month we bill. We do not bill in the school holidays. The first month is free.

Setup and training

Setup, data capture and training are free.

Your data

The school owns its data and can export it at any time. Our data ownership page sets out how.

Working without internet

Campus works without internet. Work saved on a device syncs when the connection returns.

Ending the agreement

Either side can end the agreement as the school's order sets out. The school keeps its export.

Changes to these terms

We tell schools before these terms change.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe.
