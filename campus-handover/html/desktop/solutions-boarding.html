One record

The hostel works from the same record as the classroom.

A boarder's house, room, guardians and fees sit on one record. When a pupil signs out on exeat, the house list and the duty teacher know.

Roll call

Take roll call in minutes, house by house.

Mark each boarder on a phone at prep and lights out. Anyone missing shows straight away, and roll call works without internet.

Exeats

Approve exeats and know who is out.

Keep the sick bay's log on the record.

Matron logs each visit and tells the guardian from Campus. Class teachers see who is off lessons.
