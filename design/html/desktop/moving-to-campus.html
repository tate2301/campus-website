The four weeks

From your old system to your first term on Campus.

1. Collect
We take an export from your current system, your Excel sheets or the paper registers, and agree with you what moves.
2. Map and import
Pupils, guardians, staff, fee balances and past marks go onto one record each. You check a sample before anything goes live.
3. Train
We train the office, the bursary and the staffroom at the school, and send every parent a sign-in.
4. Switch over
The bursar signs off the opening balances, you start the term on Campus, and we support you through it.
What moves

Everything the school keeps, on one record.

Pupils and guardians
Every pupil's record with their guardians, class and house.
Staff and payroll details
Contracts, leave balances and the details payroll needs.
Fee structures and opening balances
Each family's balance in US dollars and ZiG, signed off by the bursar.
Marks and past reports
This year's marks and the reports already sent home.
Classes and timetables
Forms, subjects, rooms and the timetable for the term.
Stock lists
Stores, uniforms and textbooks, with what is issued to whom.
Questions

What schools ask before they move.

Do we stop using our old system first?
No. Keep it running until go-live. After that it stays readable for as long as you need it.
Our records are on paper. Can you still move them?
Yes. We capture them, and data capture is part of the free setup.
Who checks the figures?
You do. The bursar signs off every opening balance and the Head signs off the class lists before go-live.
What does moving cost?
Nothing. Setup, data capture and training are free, and the first month of service is free.

Running in four weeks.

Two weeks to set up and two weeks of training at your school. Then we support you through the rest of the term.
